### NEW TOPICS NOT IN TAXONOMY

#### NEW:slide_errors_and_typos (Estimated Count: 30 comments)
**The audience frequently identifies editing blunders, visual typos, and mathematical inconsistencies on the presentation slides.** Viewers point out that slides mislabel "short" legs as "long" (such as in the Apple calendar spread), display "puts" when discussing "calls," label basic vertical spreads as "Iron Condors," and output incorrect math for max risk calculations (such as stating a \$9,450 max loss on a 10-contract 5-wide spread instead of \$4,450). Commenters express frustration that these errors confuse novices who are trying to reconcile the presenter's speech with the on-screen graphics.

#### NEW:underperformance_vs_hold (Estimated Count: 25 comments)
**Viewers systematically demonstrate that active options income campaigns (like selling covered calls or running the Wheel on SPY/QQQ) consistently underperformed a simple buy-and-hold strategy during bullish years.** Commenters provide exact price math proving that buying shares outright on Day 1 and doing nothing generated significantly higher net profits, avoided active management stress, and qualified for long-term capital gains tax treatment instead of heavily taxed short-term options premium cash flows.

#### NEW:pnl_graphs (Estimated Count: 15 comments)
**The public strongly requests that the presenters include visual risk payoff diagrams (P&L curves mapping underlying asset price to profit/loss) rather than relying solely on text-heavy PowerPoint tables.** Visual learners note that options strategies with multiple legs are notoriously difficult to conceptualize in text, and displaying the risk profile graph on screen would instantly clarify breakeven boundaries and the cliff-like nature of maximum loss zones.
