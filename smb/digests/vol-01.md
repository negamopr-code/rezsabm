One trader made \$3,000 in two weeks on a new strategy, got cocky and quadrupled trade size, and subsequently lost \$2,000 in a day and revenge-traded to a negative \$4,000 loss over the following two weeks [#31 👍45 2020-08-26] [2]. Another viewer made \$150,000 in a single month only to lose \$40,000 on the third day of the next month due to increasing trade sizes [#101 👍12 2021-08-26] [3].

2. ⭐ **Viewers find building the "risk tolerance muscle" to be their hardest chronic challenge, reporting that their biggest losses stem from the emotional pull of "feel better" decisions like bailing out of winning trades too early or widening stops on losing ones.** Under Video 1 (uploaded 2019-10-31), over 30 comments discuss this emotional asymmetry [1]. While viewers appreciate the quote that risk tolerance is a muscle that must be built [#2 👍474 2020-08-26] [4], many admit that getting out of positions and controlling FOMO are their main points of failure [#2.4 👍0 2022-08-26; #57 👍31 2021-08-26] [4, 5], with some noting that they get emotional and "fiddle" with stops when staring at active trades [#109 👍5 2022-08-26] [6].

### Product/Course/Tool Recommendations

1. **A Rules-Based Position-Sizing and Stop Simulator**: To directly address "Superman syndrome" and the emotional struggle of bailing out of trades [2, 3], the product must feature an automated simulator that locks users out of trading if they break capital-sizing rules (such as risking over 2% of their starting capital) [10]. It should train "risk tolerance as a muscle" by gamifying disciplined stops and punishing "fiddling" with active trade parameters [1, 24].
2. **An Honest "All-Weather" Scenario Planner and Commission Calculator**: Any educational calculator must feature a multi-scenario simulator that clearly outlines the worst-case loss potential (such as 100% loss [13], sharp stock depreciation [25], or sudden early assignment [14]) and integrates drag-and-drop commission models [10, 15]. Calculations must automatically subtract bid-ask spreads and the cost of buying back in-the-money short options to display authentic net profits [10, 11].
3. **The "Micro-Account" Brokerage and PDT Navigation Guide**: A dedicated onboarding module must show traders with accounts under \$1,000 [4] exactly which low-barrier brokerages to use to get Tier 3 spread approvals [9]. It should teach tactical workarounds to avoid pattern day trader (PDT) violations [9] or show how to convert to unlimited-day-trade cash accounts using strategies like cash-secured puts rather than restricted margin accounts [26, 27].
