### NEW TOPICS NOT IN TAXONOMY

#### NEW:buy_and_hold_outperformance (Estimated Count: 150 comments)
A recurring mathematical critique from the audience is that the active options strategies presented—such as the monthly Wheel or diagonal income campaigns—frequently underperformed simply buying and holding the underlying ETF (like SPY or QQQ) or stock (like TSLA) over the same backtested period. Commenters outline that passive buy-and-hold investing yields larger capital gains, requires zero management time, carries no options commission friction, and avoids the heavy short-term capital gains tax drag associated with options premium income.

#### NEW:slide_errors_and_typos (Estimated Count: 140 comments)
Audience members frequently identify structural errors in the visual graphics of the presentation slides. These include labels showing "puts" instead of "calls" on risk diagrams, mismatched numbers between the slides and the actual live trading platform window, and copy-paste errors where tables from previous videos were reused without updating the text descriptions. Viewers express frustration that these errors confuse beginners who are trying to match the presenter's spoken narrative with the on-screen text.

#### NEW:repetitive_basics (Estimated Count: 10 comments)
Experienced options traders in the audience frequently request that the channel stop explaining basic options definitions (such as "what is a call" or "what is a spread") at the beginning of every advanced strategy video. While some acknowledge that this is helpful for first-time viewers, advanced traders find it repetitive and urge the presenters to refer beginners to an introductory playlist so that advanced strategy videos can jump straight into high-level adjustments and risk parameters.
